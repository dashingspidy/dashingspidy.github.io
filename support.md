# Miqaat Support

  Miqaat helps you protect your prayer time, build a Quran reading habit, and
  stay focused by limiting distracting apps.

  ## Contact Support

  For help with Miqaat, email:

  **hamid_kawser@hotmail.be**

  Please include:

  - Your device model
  - Your iOS version
  - The issue you are experiencing
  - Any relevant screenshots

  Please do not send sensitive personal information.

  ## Restore Your Purchase

  To restore a previous Miqaat purchase:

  1. Open Miqaat.
  2. Open the paywall or Premium section.
  3. Tap **Restore Purchases**.
  4. Sign in with the Apple ID used to make the purchase.

  Purchases can only be restored through the same Apple ID used for the
  original purchase.

  ## Manage or Cancel a Subscription

  Apple manages Miqaat subscriptions.

  To manage or cancel your subscription:

  1. Open the **Settings** app.
  2. Tap your name.
  3. Tap **Subscriptions**.
  4. Select **Miqaat**.
  5. Manage or cancel your subscription.

  ## Prayer Times

  Miqaat can calculate prayer times using your location or a city selected
  manually.

  If prayer times appear incorrect:

  - Check your selected city.
  - Confirm your calculation method.
  - Confirm your madhab setting.
  - Make sure your device date, time, and timezone are correct.

  ## Location Access

  Location access is optional.

  When enabled, Miqaat uses your location to calculate prayer times. Your
  location is processed for this purpose and is not uploaded to Miqaat
  servers.

  You can change location access in:

  **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Miqaat**

  ## Notifications

  Miqaat uses local notifications for prayer and Quran reading reminders.

  If reminders are not appearing:

  1. Open **Settings**.
  2. Tap **Notifications**.
  3. Select **Miqaat**.
  4. Enable **Allow Notifications**.
  5. Confirm that your reminder settings are enabled inside Miqaat.

  ## App Blocking

  Miqaat uses Apple’s Screen Time and Family Controls features to block
  selected apps during prayer or Quran reading periods.

  If app blocking is not working:

  - Confirm that Screen Time access was granted.
  - Confirm that app blocking is enabled in Miqaat.
  - Confirm that apps were selected.
  - Open Miqaat again to refresh the blocking schedule.
  - Make sure notifications are enabled so Miqaat can maintain its schedule.

  ## Quran Reading

  Your Quran reading goal, progress, reading time, and Quran script
  preference are stored on your device.

  If your reading progress appears incorrect, close and reopen Miqaat. If the
  issue continues, contact support.

  ## Challenges

  Challenges are completed manually. Open the Challenges section and confirm
  your completion for the current day.

  Challenge progress is stored locally on your device.

  ## Privacy

  Miqaat does not require an account. Prayer history, Quran progress, goals,
  and challenge activity are stored locally on your device.

  Read our [Privacy Policy](./privacy.md).
